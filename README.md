Ceflow
AI-powered yield optimization on Celo.
Ceflow is an AI-powered DeFi yield agent built for the Celo ecosystem. It helps users discover the best stablecoin yield opportunities, analyze portfolio performance, compare protocols, and execute deposits or withdrawals through a single, intelligent dashboard.
Rather than chasing the highest APY alone, Ceflow evaluates yield opportunities based on multiple factors including risk, protocol trust, liquidity, withdrawal flexibility, and live market data to recommend the most suitable strategy for each user.

How It Works
Connect your Celo wallet.
View your portfolio balances.
Fetch live yield opportunities.
Analyze your portfolio with the AI engine.
Compare protocols based on APY, risk, liquidity, and trust.
Receive personalized recommendations.
Prepare transactions directly from the dashboard.
Approve transactions in your wallet.
Monitor your positions and portfolio performance over time.


⸻


AI Analysis Engine
Ceflow includes an intelligent analysis layer capable of:
Portfolio optimization
Opportunity ranking
Risk assessment
Health scoring
Scenario simulation
Yield forecasting
Personalized recommendations
Automated monitoring
The AI assists with decision-making but never signs or executes transactions on behalf of the user. Every transaction requires explicit wallet approval.
